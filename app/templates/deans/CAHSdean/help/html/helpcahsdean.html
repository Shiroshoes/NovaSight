Help Center

This Help Center covers what's available to CAHS Dean accounts: viewing dashboards and predictions for your college. Uploading grade data and managing user accounts are handled by Academic Affairs, MISO and Admin.

Your College

Your College Only

As a CAHS Dean, every number, chart and table in NovaSight covers CAHS only. There is no college filter to change, and other colleges' data is not available to your account. You can still narrow a card to one course or year level with its filter.

Dashboards

Dashboards

Two dashboards are available from the sidebar, both for CAHS:

  • CAHS Dashboard shows recorded data only: the KPI card, Risk Heatmap, Gender & Status Breakdown and Top Hardest Subjects.
  • Prediction Analysis shows forecasts only: the KPI card, Top Hardest Subjects, GWA Trend and At-Risk Forecast.

Student records are always shown as anonymized IDs, never by name, and the dashboards show aggregates only.

Reading Prediction Analysis

Every value on the Prediction Analysis page is a forecast built from the semesters already uploaded. The note at the top tells you how many semesters the model learned from.

  • KPI card - always 1 semester ahead. Each badge compares the forecast with the last recorded semester.
  • Top Hardest Subjects (Bar Chart and Ranked Cards) - the next semester, compared with each subject's last recorded offering. The gray bar is that earlier offering and the ▲ / ▼ badge is the change (red = harder, green = easier). Grade Trend follows each subject across recorded terms and into the forecast.
  • GWA Trend and At-Risk Forecast - solid lines are recorded semesters; dashed lines and the shaded band are the forecast. GWA uses the Philippine scale, so 1.00 (best) is at the top and a line moving up means grades are improving.
  • How far ahead - the charts look 2 semesters ahead once 6 semesters are uploaded, and the range grows automatically as more semesters are added.

NovaSight is built for decision support, not evaluation. Predictions are meant to help flag student cohorts who may benefit from additional academic support. They are not a performance score for any college, department, or dean, and are never used to rank or compare colleges against each other.

Card Tools

Every dashboard card has the same icons in its top-right corner:

  • Table - opens the numbers behind the chart as a table you can search, sort, filter and download as CSV. Clicking the chart does the same.
  • Filter - pick a department, course, year level and more, then press Apply. Filters belong to their own card.
  • Refresh - puts that card's filters back to their defaults.
  • Expand - shows the card full screen. Press Esc to close it.

Download PDF (top right of a dashboard) opens a preview first. Choose which charts to include, the paper size and the orientation, and see how many pages the PDF will have before you download it. Only the charts are printed. In the print window that opens next, choose Save as PDF.

Tips

Using NovaSight on a Phone or Tablet

On a tablet or phone the sidebar is hidden to give the dashboards more room. Tap the ☰ button in the header (or swipe right from the left edge) to open it; tap outside it, press ✕, or swipe left to close it. Filters open as a panel at the bottom of the screen, wide tables scroll sideways, and on a phone each chart's description sits behind an About this chart button.

Account

Profile

Manage your account information, update your profile picture, and change your password from the Profile page.

Account Roles

Access is role-based. As a Dean, your account is for viewing dashboards and predictions; it doesn't upload data or manage users:

  • Admin creates and manages user accounts for the system, and can also upload the student grade dataset
  • Academic Affairs and MISO upload the student grade dataset that powers every prediction
  • Deans view dashboards and predictions for their own college only
  • Registrar and SASO view dashboards and predictions across all colleges
Troubleshooting

Common Issues

I can't log in.

Double-check your email and password, and confirm Caps Lock is off. If you still can't sign in, contact the system administrator to verify your account is active.

The dashboard is empty.

Make sure at least one dataset has been uploaded and processed successfully for CAHS in the selected year and semester. Uploading is handled by Academic Affairs, MISO and Admin.

A chart shows a flat line or an unrealistic jump.

Forecasts need enough history to detect a trend. With only a few semesters a forecast can look unusually flat or steep; it smooths out as more semesters are uploaded.

Prediction Analysis says “No prediction model yet”.

The prediction models need at least 6 uploaded semesters and a finished training run. Ask MISO or the Academic Affairs to confirm the data has been uploaded.

Top Hardest Subjects is empty.

The Bar Chart and Ranked Cards only list subjects that are normally offered in the semester being predicted (1st or 2nd). Clear the card's filters to widen the list.

The numbers changed after a new upload.

Forecasts are recalculated from every uploaded semester each time new data arrives, so values move slightly. That is expected.

Cards show gray shapes instead of charts.

Those placeholders appear while a card is loading. If they stay for more than a few seconds, refresh the page.

The sidebar is missing on my phone.

Tap the ☰ button at the top-left of the header to open it.

I don't see an Upload option.

Uploading grade data is limited to Academic Affairs, MISO and Admin accounts. If your role needs upload access, contact the system administrator.

Why can't I see other colleges?

Dean accounts are limited to their own college, by design. Ask the Registrar or SASO for campus-wide figures.
