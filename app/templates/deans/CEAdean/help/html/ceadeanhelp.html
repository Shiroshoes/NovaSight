Help

Dean · CEA

This Help Center covers what's available to CEA Dean accounts: viewing dashboards and predictions for your college. Uploading grade data and managing user accounts are handled by Academic Affairs and Admin.

Dashboards

The Main Dashboard gives a campus-wide view across all colleges. The CEA Dashboard narrows every chart down to your college alone — dropout risk and retention, GWA ranking and trends, INC rate, irregular vs. regular status, enrollment, and the hardest subjects.

Historical vs. Prediction Mode

Every dashboard has a mode toggle. Historical mode shows real, already-recorded data. Prediction mode switches the same charts to NovaSight's forecast for future years — look for the dashed trend line and the "Predicted" label, which always mark a forecast rather than an actual recorded value.

Profile

Manage your account information, update your profile picture, and change your password from the Profile page.

Account Roles

Access is role-based. As a Dean, your account is for viewing dashboards and predictions — it doesn't upload data or manage users:

  • Admin creates and manages user accounts for the system
  • Academic Affairs uploads the student grade dataset that powers every prediction
  • Deans, Registrar, and SASO view dashboards and predictions for their area

Common Issues

I can't log in.

Double-check your email and password, and confirm Caps Lock is off. If you still can't sign in, contact the system administrator to verify your account is active.

The dashboard is empty.

Make sure at least one dataset has been uploaded and processed successfully for CEA in the selected year and semester. Uploading is handled by Academic Affairs.

A chart shows a flat line or an unrealistic jump.

Forecast charts need enough historical years to detect a reliable trend. With only a few years of data, a forecast can look unusually flat or steep — this smooths out as more historical data is uploaded.

I don't see an Upload option.

Uploading grade data is limited to Academic Affairs accounts. If your role needs upload access, contact the system administrator.

Need Assistance?

If the problem persists, please contact the NovaSight system administrator or the development team for further assistance.
