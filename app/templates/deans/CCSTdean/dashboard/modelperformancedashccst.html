Training Errors

    Learn more
    What it shows
    Every trained model's headline score (R² for regressors, Accuracy for classifiers) plus its supporting metrics (RMSE, MAE, MSE, F1), and which ones were skipped or errored during the last training run.
    What to expect
    R²/Accuracy closer to 1.0 and RMSE/MAE closer to 0 are better. A model that's "Skipped" usually just means there isn't enough data for it yet, not that something's broken.
    What it means
    A model that's visibly worse than the others, or newly showing "Error," is worth checking before trusting its forecasts elsewhere on the dashboards. The charts below let you look inside a specific model's predictions rather than just its summary score.
