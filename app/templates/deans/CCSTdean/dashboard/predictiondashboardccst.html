Prediction window

KPI values look exactly one semester ahead of the last recorded semester, no matter how much data has been uploaded. The badge next to each number compares that forecast with the last recorded semester.

Total Enrollment

Forecast headcount of unique students, split into Regular and Irregular. Year-level chips show each year level’s share of the forecast.

Average GWA

Forecast mean General Weighted Average (Philippine scale — 1.00 is highest). A downward arrow is an improvement.

Avg Completion Rate

Forecast share of enrolled credit units that students actually earn (Units Earned ÷ Units Enrolled).

Status Breakdown

Forecast number of students with at least one Failed, Dropped (DRP), Incomplete (INC), Unofficial Drop (UDR), Withdrawn (W) or No Grade (NGA) outcome. A student with two different statuses is counted under each, so “All” can exceed the number of students affected.

Hardest Subjects Filters
Metric
Ranking

Bar Chart and Ranked Cards predict exactly one semester ahead — the next semester after the last uploaded one. Only subjects normally offered in that semester slot (1st or 2nd) are listed. The gray bar / “last recorded offering” line is the same subject’s most recent recorded offering, and the ▲ / ▼ badge is the change from it (red = harder, green = easier).

Metric

Avg Grade ranks by predicted average grade (1.00 best – 5.00 worst). Failed, INC, DRP, UDR and W rank by the predicted share of enrolled students with that status; the bar shows that percentage and how many students it is out of.

Views

Bar Chart and Ranked Cards show the same ranking two ways; Grade Trend follows each ranked subject’s average grade across recorded terms and into the forecast (dashed), for as many semesters as the Grade Trend horizon allows.

Table & Chips

Click a bar or a ranked card to open its full breakdown as a table; the small chips (F, INC, DRP, UDR, W, GWA) are that subject’s predicted outcome counts and average grade.

GWA Trend

Predicted average GWA per course — dashed = forecast

GWA Trend Filters
Lines

One line per CCST course. Solid segments are recorded semesters; dashed segments and the shaded band are the forecast.

Scale

Philippine GWA scale (1.00 is best, 5.00 is failing), and the axis is flipped so 1.00 sits at the top — a line moving up means grades are projected to improve. Semesters with no GWA (students with nearly every subject failed or carrying a status) are left out of the mean.

Horizon

How many semesters ahead the line runs is set by the Forecast horizon in the filter. The maximum grows automatically as more semesters are uploaded.

Table & Zoom

Click the chart to open the plotted values as a table. GWA moves slowly, so “Zoom y-axis to the data” magnifies small differences between lines.

At-Risk Forecast

Predicted number of students per status — dashed = forecast

At-Risk Forecast Filters
Status
What it shows

The predicted number of students with at least one subject marked with the selected status, per CCST course, for each semester in the forecast horizon.

What to expect

Counts follow both the recent risk rate and the expected headcount, so a growing program can show more at-risk students even when its rate is flat.

What it means

A line that climbs across the shaded band marks the college or course to plan support for first — compare it with its headcount in the KPI card before reading it as a worsening rate.

Table

Click the chart to open the plotted counts as a table, with a CSV download.
