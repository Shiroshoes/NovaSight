Performance by Year Level
Learn more
What it shows
Each bar is one year level (1st–4th Year, etc.), segmented by college. Hover a segment for exact headcount and its performance-band breakdown. Reflects Main Campus by default, or the selected college/course from the filter above.
What to expect
Bar heights (total headcount per year level) naturally shrink from 1st to 4th year as some students drop or stop out along the way — that funnel shape is expected, not itself a red flag.
What it means
Watch the performance-band mix inside each segment rather than just the bar height: a year level where a college's "Below Average"/"Failing" bands grow noticeably wider than its own earlier years is the more meaningful signal.
INC / Irregular / Drop Rate by Year Level
Learn more
What it shows
One rate at a time (pick INC / Irregular / Drop with the buttons above), broken down by year level and college. "Irregular Rate" here is behavioral (had an INC or dropped a subject this term) — separate from the "Irregular" registrar classification used elsewhere. Each college always uses the same color across this whole dashboard.
What to expect
All three rates are usually a small minority of students per year level; Drop Rate is typically the smallest of the three, INC Rate the largest.
What it means
A year level/college combination where any of these rates climbs well above its usual range is worth investigating — it often lines up with the same courses flagged in the Dropout Rate Heatmap.

What it shows
The count/share of students flagged Irregular — at least one failed (5.0) or dropped subject on record.
What to expect
This is normally the smaller of the two Regular/Irregular groups; a sudden jump term over term is the thing to watch for, not the raw number alone.

A rising Irregular share is an early academic-risk signal worth cross-checking against the INC/Irregular/Drop Rate by Year Level and Dropout Risk Ranking charts to see where it's concentrated.
GWA Distribution (Student Scatter)
Learn more
What it shows
Each dot is one student's GWA. Solid-colored dots are real historical terms; hollow crosshair dots are the model's forecast for future terms — see prediction-mode-visuals.js's scatter styling.
What to expect
Forecast dots should sit close to where the real-data trend was already heading — a big jump right at the real/forecast boundary is worth a second look.
What it means
A college whose forecast dots trend downward (toward 5.0) is projected to see grades slip if current patterns continue.

INC Rate Forecast (Incomplete Grades)
Learn more
What it shows
Projected percentage of students who will receive an 'INC' status. Solid line = real history; dashed line = forecast.
What to expect
INC rates tend to drift gradually rather than spike suddenly.
What it means
A college whose dashed line trends sharply upward is projected to see more Incomplete grades in the coming term(s) if current patterns hold.

Learn more
What it shows
Dropout rate per college/course over time, with a dashed forecast tail and a red dot flagging any term where the rate spiked well above its own normal range.
What to expect
Lines should move gradually; red spike dots should be rare.
What it means
A dashed line trending sharply upward, or a spike dot right at the forecast boundary, marks a college/course worth investigating.
