From term records to an early outlook

NovaSight works quietly in the background of the normal academic cycle. Three steps turn the grades the university already collects into a forward-looking view.

1

Term data goes in

Authorized staff from Academic Affairs and MISO upload each term’s grade sheets. Every file is checked for the right format and for duplicates before any of it is used.

2

Models learn the history

Once enough semesters are on record, the system trains its prediction models on the accumulated history and retrains whenever a new term is added.

3

Dashboards show the outlook

Deans, the Registrar and Academic Affairs see where risk is likely to rise by college, program and year level, each prediction shown with its own reliability.

About NovaSight

To Act As The Organization’s Lodestar by Altering Historical Data of Record into Proactive, Reliable Outlook and Prognosis that Empower Practical Judgement, Operation and Mitigating Risk

Mission

To Designed A Utility For Metamorphosis Of Complex Data of Fact In Lucid Acumen And Precise Prediction With Reliable Outlook, Prognosis, forecast

Why This Study

Academic records at BPSU — grades, enrollment status, dropouts, incompletes — are already collected every term, but they are reviewed manually and looked at after the fact, once a student has already struggled for a semester or more. The development team set out to build NovaSight after observing that colleges and departments had no shared, forward-looking view of this data: no early signal for which cohorts were trending toward higher failure, drop, or incompletion rates before the numbers were already final. The goal was a system that turns historical academic records into a proactive outlook, not just a historical record.

See how it works

What This Study Is Used For

NovaSight is built for decision support, not for scoring or ranking any college, department, or dean. It flags at-risk records and cohorts early — by status (Failed, Dropped, Incomplete, Unofficial Drop, Withdrawn), by college and program, and by year level — so that academic staff can direct intervention and advising where it is likely to matter most, instead of reviewing every record after each term closes. Every prediction is shown alongside its own reliability, rather than as a single certain number, so the dashboard is meant to inform judgment and planning, not replace it.

About

What is NovaSight?

NovaSight is the Bataan Peninsula State University (BPSU) student performance prediction system. It turns the academic records that authorized staff upload each semester into dashboards and forecasts that help flag groups of students who may benefit from extra academic support.

It is an internal tool for BPSU personnel. This public site never shows student data; everything is behind the login.

Getting Started

Logging In

  1. Select Login at the top right of any page.
  2. Enter your BPSU email (for example Account@bpsu.edu.ph) and your password. Use the eye icon to show or hide what you type.
  3. Press LOGIN.

Accounts are created only for verified BPSU personnel; there is no self-registration. If you do not have an account yet, please contact the Academic Affairs.

What You Will See After Logging In

The pages you can open depend on the role assigned to your account. Signed-in users can typically open:

  • Dashboard - recorded student performance data for your college or area of responsibility.
  • Prediction Analysis - forecasts of enrollment, GWA, at-risk counts, and the subjects expected to be hardest next semester.
  • Profile and Help - your own account details and a Help Center written for your role.
Good to Know

Predictions Are Decision Support

NovaSight is built for decision support, not evaluation. Predictions are meant to help flag student cohorts who may benefit from additional academic support. They are not a performance score for any college, department, or dean, and are never used to rank or compare colleges against each other.

Tips

Using NovaSight on a Phone or Tablet

On a tablet or phone the menu links collapse into the ☰ button at the top-right of the header. Tap it to open the menu, then tap ✕ (or any link) to close it.

Privacy

Cookies and Privacy

NovaSight uses a single session cookie that keeps you signed in after you log in. It does not track you and is never shared with third parties. The first time you visit you are asked to accept it; that choice is remembered for a year. Read the full details in the Privacy Policy.

Troubleshooting

Common Issues

I can't log in.

Enter your full BPSU email and check that Caps Lock is off; use the eye icon to make sure the password is what you meant to type. If both are correct, your account may have been deactivated - please contact the Academic Affairs.

I forgot my password.

Please contact the Academic Affairs. An administrator can set a new password for your account.

It says my role was updated and I have to log in again.

When the role on your account changes, NovaSight signs you out so that your pages match your new role. Log in again to continue.

The cookie notice keeps appearing.

Your choice is remembered with a cookie. If your browser blocks or clears cookies, the notice appears again. Accepting it again is safe.

The page looks cut off on my phone.

Refresh the page and make sure your browser is up to date. Tap the ☰ button to open the menu.

Policy

1. Who This Applies To

NovaSight is an internal academic prediction system built for Bataan Peninsula State University staff. It is not a public registration service, accounts are created only by MISO for verified BPSU personnel. This policy covers both those account holders and anyone browsing the public, no-login pages of the site (such as this one).

2. Information We Collect

Account information, for every staff account MISO creates: first name, last name, middle initial/suffix (if provided), BPSU account email, a securely hashed password (never stored in plain text), assigned role, and an optional profile picture, plus small interface settings such as the default avatar color assigned to your role or department and whether you have already seen a page's tutorial video. This information identifies real staff members and is used solely to operate their account within the system.

Uploaded academic datasets, submitted only by Academic Affairs and MISO accounts, which power every dashboard and prediction in the system. Each upload is processed as one semester. The original file, the cleaned student and grade records for that semester, and a small database record (academic year, semester, number of rows, parsing accuracy, and who uploaded it) are kept on secured, university-managed storage, accessible only to the same MISO/Academic Affairs roles authorized to upload them in the first place, for record keeping and to allow re-processing if needed. On every dashboard, individual student records are shown only by an anonymized student ID (never by name) so no student identity is exposed to deans, Registrar, or SASO through the dashboards themselves.

Trained models and forecasts. The prediction models are trained from the uploaded semesters. They store statistical parameters and aggregated figures, for example headcount, average GWA, and status rates per college, course, year level, and subject, not copies of student records. The Prediction Analysis page displays only these aggregates and never an individual student.

Session data: a single authentication cookie that keeps you signed in. It is not used for tracking, does not follow you across other sites, and carries no advertising or analytics identifiers, because NovaSight does not run any analytics, tracking, or advertising scripts of any kind.

Uploaded profile pictures are re-processed on upload, decoded, stripped of any embedded file metadata, and re-saved as a clean image before being stored, so no hidden metadata from the original file (such as device or location information some images carry) is retained.

3. How Information Is Used

Account information is used only to operate the system: authenticating logins, displaying who uploaded a dataset for audit purposes, and personalizing each account's profile. Uploaded academic data is used only to generate the dashboards, trends, and predictions NovaSight was built to provide; forecasts are recalculated from all uploaded semesters each time new data arrives. NovaSight does not sell, rent, or share any information collected here with third parties, and does not use it for any purpose beyond operating the system for BPSU.

4. Predictions Are Decision Support, Not Evaluation

NovaSight's predictions are built to help flag student cohorts who may benefit from additional academic support. They are not a performance score for any college, department, or dean, and information collected through this system is never used to rank or compare colleges against one another.

5. How Information Is Protected

  • Passwords are never stored in plain text, only a one-way secure hash.
  • Student records are referenced only by an anonymized ID on every dashboard, never by name, and forecast pages show aggregated figures only.
  • Login sessions are transmitted only over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  • Access to uploading academic data and to the admin account-management tools is restricted by role; most accounts can only view dashboards, not modify data or other users.
  • Regular backups are kept so academic records and account data are not lost to a technical failure.

6. Third-Party Resources

To draw charts and display fonts, some pages load open-source libraries (Chart.js and its labels plugin) from the jsDelivr content delivery network and typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser requests these files, those providers can see standard connection details such as your IP address, browser type, and the page that requested the file. No account details, student records, or academic data are sent to them, and NovaSight does not use them for analytics or advertising.

7. Data Retention

Account and academic dataset records are retained for as long as needed to support BPSU's academic reporting and record-keeping requirements. Deactivated ("archived") accounts are retained rather than immediately erased, so historical audit trails (such as who uploaded a given dataset) remain intact. Prediction models are recalculated from the stored semester records whenever new data is uploaded. If you wish to have your account deactivated, please contact the Academic Affairs.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Account holders can update their profile picture and password at any time from their Profile page. For any other change, such as correcting account details, deactivating an account, or questions about what information is held, please contact the Academic Affairs.

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 you may also have the right to be informed about how your personal data is processed, to access and correct it, to object to its processing, to request its erasure or blocking where the law allows, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change accordingly. Significant changes will be communicated to account holders directly where practical.
