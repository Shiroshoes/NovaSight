NovaSight

What’s waiting behind the login

A decision-support dashboard that turns the grades the university already collects into an early, forward-looking outlook for advising and planning.

  • Dashboards & predictions

    Enrollment, GWA and at-risk trends by college, program and year level, with forecasts for the coming terms.

  • Anonymous by design

    Records are referenced only by an anonymized student ID, never by name, so no identity is exposed.

  • Honest about reliability

    Each prediction is shown with its own reliability instead of a single, falsely certain number.

  • Always current

    Models are first trained once 6 semesters of academic data are uploaded, then retrained after every 2 new semesters.

Access for
  • Academic Affairs
  • MISO
  • Registrar
  • College Deans

NovaSight is decision support, not a scorecard. It is never used to score or rank any college, department or dean. Signing in uses a single session cookie that keeps you logged in — no tracking, no analytics.
