Heatmap Filters

Cell Value

Percentage: students with the selected status ÷ students enrolled in that course and year level. No. of Students: how many students that is. A dash means no enrolled students.

Colour Scale

Colour shows the share of enrolled students in that course and year level who have the selected status — green is low, dark red is 100%. It is the same in both views, so a cell keeps its colour when you switch.

Status

Failed, Incomplete (INC), Dropped (DRP), Withdrawn (W) or Unofficial Drop (UDR) — switch between them with the Status buttons above the heatmap.

Row Order

Courses are ranked by their combined rate across all year levels; flip the direction with the sort selector in the header.

Top Hardest Subjects

Ranked by failure density — bar chart, ranked cards, and grade trend

Hardest Subjects Filters
Metric
Ranking

Subjects ranked by the selected Metric among students with a final grade this semester; Top N and Sort order in the filter control how many are shown and in which direction.

Metric

Avg Grade is a share/average across the subject's students; Failed, INC, DRP, UDR and W are raw counts of that outcome.

Views

Bar Chart and Ranked Cards show the same ranking two ways; Grade Trend follows the ranked subjects' average grade across academic years and semesters.

Table & Chips

Click a bar or a ranked card to open its full breakdown as a table; the small chips (F, INC, DRP, UDR, W, GWA) are that subject's outcome counts and average grade.
