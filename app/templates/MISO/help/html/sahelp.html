HELP CENTER

MISO

This Help Center covers the tools available to MISO: uploading student grade data and reading the resulting dashboards and forecasts.

Profile

Manage your account information, update your profile picture, and change your password from the Profile page.

Upload File

Upload the official student grade Excel file from the Upload File page, one file per semester. NovaSight cleans and stores each file, and once at least 6 semesters (3 school years) have been uploaded it retrains its prediction models and refreshes every dashboard automatically.

Accepted File

  • .xlsx (Microsoft Excel) only. The .xls, .csv, and .xlsm are not accepted

Required File Name Format

Your file must be saved with this exact name pattern (spaces or underscores both work) or the upload will be rejected before processing even starts:

YYYY-N Student-Performance Dataset.xlsx
  • YYYY - the year the semester starts (e.g. 2024)
  • N - 1 for 1st semester, 2 for 2nd semester

Examples: 2024-1 Student-Performance Dataset.xlsx, 2024-2_Student-Performance_Dataset.xlsx

Sheet Layout Inside the File

Each sheet tab is one college (e.g. CAHS-SON, CBA, CCST), containing the program name, a semester/year header, and a repeating block per student: a row of subject codes followed by that student's row of grades. Files that don't follow this layout, or that are still open in another program, will fail to process even if the name is correct.

After an Upload

  • A file that was already uploaded is detected as a duplicate.
  • When processing finishes, a training summary lists every model as Trained, Skipped or Error.
  • The more semesters you upload, the further ahead the Prediction Analysis charts can look.

Dashboards

Two dashboards are available from the sidebar:

  • Dashboard (Overview) shows recorded data only: the KPI card, Risk Heatmap, Gender & Status Breakdown and Top Hardest Subjects.
  • Prediction Analysis (Machine Learning & Prediction) shows forecasts only: the KPI card, Top Hardest Subjects, GWA Trend and At-Risk Forecast.

Reading Prediction Analysis

Every value on this page is a forecast built from the semesters already uploaded. The note at the top tells you how many semesters the model learned from.

  • KPI card - always 1 semester ahead. Each badge compares the forecast with the last recorded semester.
  • Top Hardest Subjects (Bar Chart and Ranked Cards) - the next semester, compared with each subject's last recorded offering. The gray bar is that earlier offering and the ▲ / ▼ badge is the change (red = harder, green = easier). Only subjects normally offered in that semester (1st or 2nd) are listed. Grade Trend follows each subject across recorded terms and into the forecast.
  • GWA Trend and At-Risk Forecast - solid lines are recorded semesters; dashed lines and the shaded band are the forecast. GWA uses the Philippine scale, so 1.00 (best) is at the top and a line moving up means grades are improving.
  • How far ahead - the charts look 2 semesters ahead once 6 semesters are uploaded, and the range grows automatically as more semesters are added. Use the Forecast horizon in a card's filter to shorten it.

Card Tools

Every dashboard card has the same icons in its top-right corner:

  • Table - opens the numbers behind the chart as a table you can search, sort, filter and download as CSV. Clicking the chart does the same.
  • Filter - pick a department, course, year level and more, then press Apply. Filters belong to their own card.
  • Refresh - puts that card's filters back to their defaults.
  • Expand - shows the card full screen. Press Esc to close it.

Download PDF (top right of a dashboard) opens a preview first. Choose which charts to include, the paper size and the orientation, and see how many pages the PDF will have before you download it. Only the charts are printed. In the print window that opens next, choose Save as PDF.

Using NovaSight on a Phone or Tablet

On a tablet or phone the sidebar is hidden to give the dashboards more room. Tap the ☰ button in the header (or swipe right from the left edge) to open it; tap outside it, press ✕, or swipe left to close it. Filters open as a panel at the bottom of the screen, wide tables scroll sideways, and on a phone each chart's description sits behind an About this chart button.

Model Training Results

After each upload, a training summary shows every model labeled Trained, Skipped (not enough data yet), or Error (the message explains what went wrong), so you can tell at a glance whether a forecast is backed by a trained model.

NovaSight is built for decision support, not evaluation. Predictions are meant to help flag student cohorts who may benefit from additional academic support. They are not a performance score for any college, department, or dean, and are never used to rank or compare colleges against each other.

Common Issues

My Excel file won't upload.

Confirm the file is .xlsx, that the file name matches the required format exactly, and that it isn't still open in Excel.

No prediction appears after uploading.

The models need at least 6 uploaded semesters with complete student information and valid grades, and a finished training run. Check the training summary: every model should read Trained.

Prediction Analysis says “No prediction model yet”.

Fewer than 6 semesters have been uploaded, or training has not finished yet. Upload the remaining semesters and check the training summary.

Top Hardest Subjects is empty on Prediction Analysis.

The Bar Chart and Ranked Cards only list subjects that are normally offered in the semester being predicted (1st or 2nd). Clear the filters to widen the list, and check that the subject model shows Trained in the training summary.

A chart shows a flat line or an unrealistic jump.

Forecasts need enough history to detect a trend. With only a few semesters a forecast can look unusually flat or steep; it smooths out as more semesters are uploaded.

Cards show gray shapes instead of charts.

Those placeholders appear while a card is loading. If they stay for more than a few seconds, refresh the page; if a message replaces them, it explains what is missing.

I can't log in.

Double-check your email and password, and confirm Caps Lock is off. If you still can't sign in, contact the system administrator to verify your account is active.

Need Assistance?

If the problem persists, please contact the NovaSight system administrator or the development team for further assistance.
