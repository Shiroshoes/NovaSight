GWA Distribution (Student Scatter) (Loading...)
What it shows
Each dot is one student. Dots near the top = better grades (closer to 1.0). Dot color shows the student's course (see legend above). The dashed line is the overall average.
What to expect
A dense cluster near the average line with a thinner scatter of dots trailing toward the bottom (worse grades) is the typical shape — most students perform close to average, with a smaller tail struggling more.
What it means
A course whose dots sit consistently below the dashed average line, or a cluster of forecast points trending downward in Prediction mode, is worth a closer look at that program specifically.
Performance by Year Level
What it shows
Each bar (one year level) is segmented by course inside the selected department. Hover a segment for exact headcount and its performance-band mix (Excellent → Failing).
What to expect
Bar heights naturally shrink from 1st to 4th year as some students drop or stop out along the way — that funnel shape is expected, not itself a red flag.
What it means
Watch the performance-band mix inside each segment rather than just the bar height: a year level where a course's "Below Average"/"Failing" bands grow noticeably wider than its own earlier years is the more meaningful signal.
INC / Irregular / Drop Rate by Year Level
What it shows
One rate at a time (pick INC / Irregular / Drop with the buttons above), broken down by year level and course. "Irregular Rate" here is behavioral (had an INC or dropped a subject this term) — separate from the "Irregular" registrar classification used elsewhere.
What to expect
All three rates are usually a small minority of students per year level; Drop Rate is typically the smallest of the three, INC Rate the largest.
What it means
A year level/course combination where any of these rates climbs well above its usual range is worth investigating — it often lines up with the same courses flagged in the Dropout Rate Heatmap.

Dropout Trend & Spike Detection
Red Dot = High Risk Spike
What it shows
Each line is one course in the selected department, always shown in that course's own color. A solid line is real historical data; a dashed line is the model's future prediction. A red dot flags a term where a course's dropout rate spiked well above its own normal range.
What to expect
Lines should move gradually term to term; red spike dots should be rare, not a fixture on every line.
What it means
A red spike dot, or a dashed forecast line trending sharply upward, marks a course worth investigating for that specific term — as an early-warning signal, not a guarantee.
INC Rate Forecast (Incomplete Grades)
What it shows
Predicts the percentage of students in the selected department who will receive an 'INC' status, based on college trends. Each line is one course, always shown in that course's own color. A solid line is real historical data; a dashed line is the model's future prediction.
What to expect
INC rates tend to move gradually term to term rather than spike suddenly — a slow upward or downward drift is normal to see in the forecast portion.
What it means
A course whose dashed (forecast) line trends sharply upward is projected to see more Incomplete grades in the coming term(s) if current patterns hold — useful as an early-warning signal, not a guarantee.
